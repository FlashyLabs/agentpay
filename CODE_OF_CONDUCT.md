# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone participating here — in issues, pull requests, reviews and
discussion — is expected to be respectful, direct and honest. Disagree with
the work, not the person. Report what you measured, including when it is
worse than expected. Assume competence; ask before assuming intent.

## Unacceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others'
private information, and sustained disruption of discussion. So is passing
off a guess as a measurement — this repository's whole subject is the
difference.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the maintainers through the address in
`SECURITY.md` (the same route, deliberately: one door). Reports are handled
privately. Maintainers may edit, remove or refuse contributions and
participation that do not meet this standard.
